About EduStark

A global education services company built on relationships

EduStark delivers international student recruitment solutions through targeted high school campus visits and counsellor engagement. We connect higher-education institutions with right-fit schools, counsellors and prospective students across key international markets — helping universities build meaningful, sustainable recruitment pipelines.

What We Do

Recruitment that's planned, local and student-centric

Our approach is centred on relationship-driven recruitment, deep local market understanding and carefully planned institutional engagement.

High School Campus Visits

12–15 curated school visits per tour, placing accredited institutions directly in front of right-fit students and their counsellors.

Counsellor Engagement

Dedicated counsellor network sessions, lunches and dinners that build the trusted relationships recruitment depends on.

B2B Partner Meetings

Structured B2B meetings with education partners and agents in every market, extending your reach beyond the classroom.

Student Interaction Sessions

Face-to-face sessions where prospective students explore your programs, campus life and global pathways.

Local Market Intelligence

Initiatives informed by regional education systems, student aspirations, counsellor networks and local market dynamics.

How a Smart Tour Works

One flat fee. Everything handled.

Every EduStark tour follows a consistent, proven format so you can focus on students — not logistics.

  • 12–15 high school visits per tour
  • 2 B2B meetings with education partners
  • Student interaction & counsellor engagement sessions
  • 4/5-star accommodation, breakfast & airport transfers
  • Ground transportation & a dedicated tour leader
